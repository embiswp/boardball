This is a web implementation of the game called boardball. It is played on a board, consisting of n*m squares (n rows, m columns, n is odd and > 1, m is odd and >5). The game starts with a ball in the middle square. Two players play consecutively. The first whole column is the goalline of the first player, the last whole column is the goalline of the second player. The player wins who brings the ball to the other player's goalline. The ball can be moved so that it jumps over a plaer-stone or multiple player-stones that are next to it to the right, to the left, or diagonally. The players are putting their player-stones on the board one by one. A step of the player is either putting a player-stone on the board or jumping with the ball. Putting a player-stone on the board: there is a common stack of a predefined amount of player-stones, the players can take one player-stone from this group (while this group is not empty), and putting this on the board on a square that is empty. Jumping with the ball: jumping the player-stone or multiple player-stones. If the  jump sequence can be continued, it has to be continued. All the player-stones that were jumped over, are coming off the field, back into the common player-stone stack.
